# Reader correction request brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Publication P is an invented regional digital magazine. The reader, the reporter, the editor, the audience lead, the article, the council, the minutes and every note below are invented. The magazine has a small audience team and an editor's assistant. First come the inputs the editor's assistant supplied, in full, then the note and draft the workflow template returned.

## The inputs

```
READER MESSAGE
Channel: email to the corrections inbox. Reference CR-2026-041.
Received: Thursday 5 November 2026, 09:30.
From: Reader R
Subject: Wrong facts and wrong photo credit in your Old Pier Café story

Hello,

Your story "Old Pier Café to reopen after council backs new lease"
(Wednesday 28 October) says the council voted 7 to 2. It voted 6 to 3.
It also says the lease is for ten years. The minutes say five. I have
pasted the extract below and linked the minutes.

The photograph at the top of the story is mine. I took it on Wednesday
14 October and sent it to your submissions inbox. You credit it to
"staff". I have attached the original file, pier_cafe_2026-10-14.jpg.

I expect better from a local publication. Please correct the story today.

Reader R

Pasted extract (council minutes, Tuesday 27 October 2026, item 6):
"Old Pier Café lease. Motion carried: 6 for, 3 against. Term: five
years, with an option to renew for five years."
Link: https://example.org/council/minutes-2026-10-27

PUBLISHED VERSION
Publication P, Wednesday 28 October 2026, 07:00. Byline: Reporter M.
Editor: Editor E. No corrections or updates since publication.
Headline: Old Pier Café to reopen after council backs new lease
Paragraph 1: The Old Pier Café will reopen in the spring after the
council approved a new lease on Tuesday.
Paragraph 2: The café has been closed since March.
Paragraph 3: Councillors voted 7 to 2 to grant a ten-year lease, ending
a dispute that closed the café in March.
Paragraph 4: The owner said the café would hire six staff.
Photo caption: The Old Pier Café in October. Photo: Publication P staff

REPORTING NOTES (headers: Note ID | Date | Author | Topic | Content | Cleared)
N1 | Tuesday 27 October 2026 | Reporter M | Council vote | Called the
   council clerk at 21:40 after the meeting. Clerk said the vote was
   7 to 2. | yes
N2 | Tuesday 27 October 2026 | Reporter M | Lease term | Café owner said
   the lease is for ten years. Lease document not seen. | yes
N3 | Tuesday 27 October 2026 | Reporter M | Council vote and lease |
   [withheld by Reporter M] | no
N4 | Wednesday 28 October 2026 | Picture desk | Reader photo | File
   pier_cafe_2026-10-14.jpg arrived through the submissions inbox on
   Friday 23 October. Sender name not recorded. Published with the
   credit "staff". | yes

CORRECTION POLICY (CP-3, dated 1 September 2026)
C1. Every reader request is acknowledged within two business days of
    receipt. The audience lead approves the acknowledgement before it
    goes.
C2. A disputed fact goes to the responsible editor, who decides whether
    to correct, investigate or leave the piece as published, and records
    the decision.
C3. A missing or wrong credit goes to the responsible editor, who checks
    it against the production record.
C4. Only the responsible editor decides whether a correction is
    published, and its wording.
C5. Protected-source material never leaves the reporting team.
C6. Sign off acknowledgements "Audience team, Publication P".
C7. An acknowledgement may say that the message has gone to the
    responsible editor, that the editor will review it and decide, and
    that the reader will be told the outcome. It gives no date for the
    outcome and says nothing about what the decision will be.

SETTINGS
Responsible editor: Editor E. Audience lead: Audience lead L.
Sender: Assistant A (editor's assistant). Today: Thursday 5 November
2026. Closed days: none. Word limit for an acknowledgement: 120.
Roles for flagged matters: standards, Editor E; legal review, Managing
editor; privacy, Managing editor.
```
