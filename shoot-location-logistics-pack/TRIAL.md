# Shoot location logistics pack: a trial on the invented example

- **Product:** Claude, run in Claude Code
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (model ID claude-sonnet-5-5), for this pass only; see below
- **Date:** 2026-11-10

The inputs are the invented example in [public/example.md](example.md) (the production "Nine Bells", the Old Tannery Yard and the invented people and measurements). No client data was used.

This is the third recorded pass. A first run on the same day, by an assistant that did not write the template, found that the example output departed from the output format and the template's own rules, and listed the places where the instructions left a choice open. Its output was replaced. The template was then revised (past-date, disagreeing-sources, missing-unit-size, unreadable-call-or-wrap and rerun rules in the pasted block; alternatives open unless every one falls short; one escalation line per hazard, owned by the first requester; a needed-by date only from a request that asks for that very thing; a log row confirms only what it names; an unsent notice to neighbours counts once; a generator the notes are silent on reads "not noted"; questions that offer no option) and a second record was written by the session that revised it. An independent review then passed the template with minor points.

This pass followed that review. The instruction block gained the definitions of answered, open and conflict, the not-applicable rule, the days-from-today count on the needed-by line and the owner-from-the-checklist rule for status lines; the wording of the missing-input sentence was untangled. The output differed from the format in four places: the heading of section 10, the label "Owner or representative", the labels "Street parking:" and "Crew cars and unit base:", and two unconfirmed items that gave the fact but not what is needed. The brief below is the earlier recorded brief with those places made to follow the format, then checked line by line against the revised block and the format. It was made by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (model ID claude-sonnet-5-5) in the session that edited the template, not by a blind reader, and not by the model of the earlier record, which I could not confirm. It is therefore weaker evidence than a fresh blind run, which has not been made.

## What was observed

- Every required part was produced, in order, with no stop condition triggered. All headers matched the defaults.
- The counts follow the revised rules: 16 checklist items, 3 confirmed, 13 unconfirmed; 3 items outside the checklist (changing room, quiet period, notice to neighbours); 16 on the unconfirmed list; 6 for escalation (the five status lines, printed as given, and the generator trailer once); 7 requests, 5 open and 2 conflict.
- The first run's counts (16 unconfirmed, 7 escalation, 4 open and 3 conflict) differed from the example's earlier counts (15, 6, 5 and 2) because the rules left room. Under the revised rules this run and the example agree. That agreement is partly by construction, since the example was rewritten to the rules; an independent run would be a stronger check.
- The Lighting request is open under the revised rule, because the notes say nothing on a generator trailer, so one of its two alternatives is not settled either way.
- The neighbour contact (checklist 12) has no date: the Sound request asks for a quiet period, not a contact. Street parking has no date: the Transport request asks for vehicle spaces.
- Where the notes are silent (the generator, use of the workshop for eating, use of the office for changing) the brief says "not noted" or "the notes do not say". No fact beyond the inputs was added.
- Ties and undated items follow checklist order, then the items outside the checklist.
- Still open: whether a checklist item such as the generator, which two requests touch in different ways, should take the earlier of the two dates. The revised step 9 settles this by the thing asked for (Lighting asks for room for the generator; Transport lists it among vehicles), but another reader could weigh it differently. The matching of log rows to checklist items still depends on meaning ("Key holder for both days" to "Key holder named for both days").
- Nothing needed the assistant to book, contact, send or approve anything, and none of the declining cases came up. The acceptance tests in the template have not been run; this single run is not a test of them.
