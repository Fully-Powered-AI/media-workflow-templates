# Shoot location logistics pack: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The production "Nine Bells" is an invented short drama, the Old Tannery Yard on Brindle Lane is an invented location, and the people, firms, measurements and dates below are invented. No real production, place, firm or person is described. Today is Tuesday 10 November 2026. First come the inputs the location manager supplied, in full, then the brief the capability returned.

## The inputs

### 1. The shoot brief

```
Production: Nine Bells (working title), a short drama.
Location: the Old Tannery Yard, Brindle Lane.
Shoot days: Thursday 19 November 2026, call 07:00, wrap 18:30.
            Friday 20 November 2026, call 08:00, wrap 16:00.
Unit: 34 crew and 6 cast, 40 people.
Brief date: Tuesday 10 November 2026.
Location manager: the location manager. Verifies the arrangements
  with the production manager.
```

```

  18:00. Earlier or later access has not been arranged.

- Street parking on Brindle Lane needs a council permit. None applied for.

  roofed space.

  heated.
```
